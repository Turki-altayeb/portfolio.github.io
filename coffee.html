Project Overview

This project was built as the final capstone of a data analysis bootcamp. The goal was to analyze sales data for a specialty coffee store — covering total sales, order volumes, product performance, city-level comparisons, and the impact of discounts on revenue. The result is a fully interactive Power BI dashboard with a modern interface.

Key Insights
  • 01 Total sales exceeded 630K — indicating strong overall demand across all product categories.
  • 02 Colombian coffee topped the sales chart as the highest-selling product across all cities.
  • 03 Hail City recorded the highest performance in both total sales and quantities sold compared to all other cities.
  • 04 Discount analysis revealed that some discounted products did not lead to proportional revenue growth, suggesting a need to optimize the discount strategy.
  • 05 Sales trends over time showed clear seasonal patterns that can be used to plan inventory and promotions in advance.
